DAFA makes specialized products and total solutions that seal, absorb and protect, for wind energy, building materials and industry. The identity is orderly and monochrome: one color at a time, bold headlines on calm grounds, documentary photography, and the logo rings as the only ornament. Everything here comes from the DAFA Corporate Visual Identity guide, version 2.0 (2022), unless a line says "provisional".

## Voice

Write as DAFA is: genuine, knowledgeable, accessible, collaborative.

- **Simple and direct.** Uncomplicated words, short sentences, get to the point quickly.
- **Human.** At eye level with the customer, whatever their trade. It must be obvious there are people behind the products.
- **Worthy and self-conscious.** Grounded; show interest in the customer's challenge and support it.
- The business language is **US English**. E-mail greetings appear in the local language and in English ("Venlig hilsen / Best regards").
- Always write **DAFA** in capitals (it abbreviates "DAnsk Tætningsliste FAbrik"). Use the official company names exactly: DAFA Group A/S, DAFA A/S, DAFA Building Solutions A/S, DAFA Sverige AB, DAFA Deutschland GmbH, DAFA Sealing Technology (Tianjin) Co., Ltd., DAFA US Inc., DAFA Polska sp. z o.o., DAFA Italia S.r.l.
- The copyright notice is `©[year] DAFA A/S`, at the bottom of the last page.
- No emoji. Sentence case in headlines.

Approved taglines, used verbatim:

> The invisible lines that make wonders happen
> Whatever the industry - it's all about the sealing
> Close to the markets – close to you
> Innovative solutions – based upon solid experience
> Sustainability happens in cooperation
> New challenges lead to new accomplishments

## Logo

The files are in the Logos asset group; never redraw, recolor, rotate or stretch them, and never show the wordmark without its rings.

| Use | File |
|---|---|
| Default on white and light grounds | `dafa-logo-primary-gray.svg` or `dafa-logo-primary-blue.svg` |
| Dark grounds, segment colors and most photographs | `dafa-logo-negative.svg` |
| Stamps and one-color print where neither of the above works | `dafa-logo-black.svg` |
| One-color print only (tape, packaging, embroidery) | `dafa-logo-one-color-blue.svg` |
| Following pages of a letter, top-right of slides | `dafa-rings-gray.svg`, `dafa-rings-white.svg` |

- Keep clear space around the logo: at least the width of the rings left and right, and the height of the rings above and below.
- Do not place a colored logo on an image unless it is clearly legible; use the negative logo.
- In an e-mail signature the logo is 100px wide.
- Add ® only where DAFA holds the registration; otherwise TM.

## Color

Work **monochrome**: one publication, page or presentation carries one continuous color plus the secondary colors. Mix segment colors only to tell areas apart.

- **Corporate:** `logo-gray-100` and `logo-blue`. Use them for anything that speaks for the whole group. Tints `logo-gray-75`, `logo-gray-50`, `logo-gray-25` for panels, charts and backgrounds.
- **Segments:** `wind-100` for Wind, `building-100` for Building Materials, `industry-100` for Industry, each with 75/50/25 tints. A segment color is the base color of that segment's material; pair it with a `logo-blue` or `logo-gray-100` band, never with another segment's color as decoration.
- **Secondary:** `ochre` and `mint`, for information design (graphs, charts), icons and to establish contrast. On covers they set the display headline on a dark ground. Keep them to display sizes: neither reaches 4.5:1 on any ground.
- **Text:** `ink` on `surface` and `surface-panel`; `on-dark` on every 100% color.
- **Themes:** Light is the guide's own: white `surface`, `logo-gray-100` text. Dark puts the page on `logo-gray-100`, the ground the guide gives the negative logo, with white text; it is for screens only and is not in the guide. The palette colors keep their values in both themes; only `surface`, `surface-panel`, `ink`, `hairline`, `action` and `on-action` change. In Dark, use `dafa-logo-negative.svg` and the `-white` icons on the page ground. Printed pieces, e-mail and Office templates stay Light.
- **Digital, provisional:** `action` is the single interactive color for links, buttons and focus rings on `surface`: the logo blue in Light, white in Dark; `on-action` labels a filled button. Do not introduce a second accent.

Stay balanced: serious without being dusty, never speckled. No gradients; depth comes from photography.

## Typography

- **Avenir Next** (`--font-sans`) is the corporate face for all marketing material, headlines and body alike. Headlines are **Bold**, body is Regular; no other weights appear in the guide. The font files are not part of this system: license and install Avenir Next, otherwise the stack falls back to Arial.
- **Arial** (`--font-office`) replaces it in Word, PowerPoint and e-mail, and wherever Avenir Next lacks a language.
- Presentation sizes: `slide-title` (48 pt bold), `slide-subtitle` (28 pt), `slide-headline` (38 pt bold), `slide-body` (16 pt).
- Document sizes: `doc-cover-headline` (36 pt bold), `doc-cover-subtext` (20 pt), `doc-headline` (20 pt bold), `doc-body` (10 pt), `letter-headline` (16 pt regular), `letter-body` (9 pt), `email-signature` (10 pt), `legal` (7 pt italic).
- Screen sizes, provisional: `display`, `headline`, `title`, `lead`, `body` (17px), `caption`, `fine-print`.
- On covers a small segment label ("Wind") sits above the headline, followed by a short dash on its own line.

## Layout, shape and depth

- Compose in flat rectangular fields: a full-bleed photograph or color block, a white margin, and a footer line. Corners are square (`radius-none`); `radius-sm` is for buttons and inputs only, `radius-full` only for the circle around an icon.
- Separate sections by changing the ground (white, a 25% tint, a 100% color) before adding rules or boxes. Where a rule is needed, 1px `hairline` on `surface` or `on-dark` on color.
- No shadows on UI or type. The only shadow in the guide is the soft one behind an employee portrait.
- Letterhead margins: 25 mm left, 15 mm right and top, address block 45 mm from the edge.
- Spacing on screen, provisional: an 8px base with `space-xxs` to `space-section`; `space-lg` inside panels, `space-section` above and below a full-width color section.
- Close every brochure, product sheet and presentation with the global footer: logo, **dafa-group.com**, then "Denmark · Sweden · Germany · China · US · Norway · Poland · Italy".

## Imagery

- **Documentary, referential, relevant.** Real DAFA products, projects, markets and people; avoid stock-like pictures; the image must support the message.
- **Color attitude.** Images echo the corporate and secondary colors: desaturate slightly, or apply a monochrome filter in the page's color. Never multicolored and speckled, never as a watermark.
- **Products** are photographed on a concrete floor, or on `logo-gray-50` when that cannot mislead; close-ups are welcome.
- **People at work** look professional, sit in their real working environment and do not look into the camera.
- **Employee portraits** are always black and white, on a uniform light background with a soft shadow on the left; business casual, single-colored shirts.

## Graphic element and icons

- The **outlined logo rings** are the one graphic element: large concentric hairline arcs running off a corner of the page, in any 100% color from the palette or in white on color.
- **Icons** are a single monochrome line, simple and centered inside a circle. Use them in `logo-gray-100`, `ochre`, `mint` or white. The Icons asset group holds the three segment icons (wind, building materials, industry) in each of those inks; they are images, so pick the file for the ink you need.
- Do not mix in another icon set, and do not use emoji or filled icons.

## Components

- `GlobalFooter`, `SegmentDivider` and `EmailSignature` recreate templates from the guide.
- `Button` is an intentional addition for digital work; the guide defines no interface controls.
